\def\TheDescription{
    The Coffee is Cold. The Tests are Failing. The Server is Live.

    It’s 2:47 AM on a Tuesday, and senior software engineer Julian Vance has been awake for 38 straight hours. Fueled entirely by stale energy drinks, extreme paranoia, and a dangerously low sense of self-preservation, Julian is one keystroke away from fixing a minor bug in the company's flagship app.

    The message from management: “Skip the staging environment. Push the test code directly to production. We announce the launch in ten minutes.”

    What follows is an adrenaline-fueled nightmare through a labyrinth of unverified dependencies, commented-out safety checks, and legacy code written by a guy who left the company in 2014 without leaving documentation. As the push goes live, Julian accidentally grants every user unlimited admin privileges, turns the billing system into a random number generator, and summons a deluge of passive-aggressive Jira tickets.

    With the CTO breathing down his neck, Julian has until sunrise to undo the damage—or fake his own internet outage and vanish forever.
}
